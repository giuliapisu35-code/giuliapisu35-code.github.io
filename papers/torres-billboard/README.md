# Art Outside the Museum: "Untitled" (Billboard of an Empty Bed) (1991)

📄 **[Read / Download Full Essay (PDF)](./OutsideMuseum_Torres.pdf)**

## Abstract & Overview

This academic essay analyzes Félix González-Torres's landmark installation *"Untitled" (Billboard of an Empty Bed)* (1991), presented as part of MoMA's *Projects 34* exhibition in New York (May 16 – June 30, 1992).

The work explores the boundaries between private experience and public reality, shifting a deeply personal image—a rumpled, unmade bed—into the public urban space across New York City.

## Key Themes

- **Private vs. Public Space:** Challenging conventional boundaries by placing intimate, personal imagery in the vast urban arena.
- **Love, Loss, and Grief:** Conceived following the death of Torres's partner, Ross Laycock, from AIDS-related complications.
- **Socio-Political Context:** Addressing the legal and political realities of privacy rights for gay couples in 1980s/90s America (e.g., *Bowers v. Hardwick*).
- **Institutional & Urban Critique:** Dispersing 24 billboards across four boroughs (Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx) and questioning the physical limits of the museum.
