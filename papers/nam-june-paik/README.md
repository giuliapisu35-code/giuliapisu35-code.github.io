# Preserving Digital Art: Case Study on Video Flag by Nam June Paik

📄 **[Read / Download Full Essay (PDF)](./Preserving_NamJunePaik.pdf)**

## Abstract & Overview

This academic case study explores the complex field of time-based media and digital art conservation, focusing on Nam June Paik's monumental installation *Video Flag* (1996). 

The paper examines the technical vulnerabilities, hardware obsolescence, and conservation strategies required to maintain born-digital installations while respecting the artist's original intent.

## Key Themes

- **Digital Conservation Challenges:** Addressing technological obsolescence, material degradation, and the shift from traditional to digital preservation ethics.
- **The Artist & Medium:** Nam June Paik's pioneering role in video art, his integration of electronic media, and his early predictions of global information networks.
- **Case Study Analysis (*Video Flag*, 1996):** Examining the 70 CRT monitor grid, 4-channel video routing, and the evolution from Laser Disc to DVD migration.
- **Preventive Interventions:** Managing extreme heat generated by CRT monitors, fire safety integration (VESDA systems), and reversible hardware upgrades at the Hirshhorn Museum.
